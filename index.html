HOW CYBERVAULT PROTECTS YOU

100% PRIVATE

CyberVault runs entirely on your device using your browser's built-in window.crypto.subtle engine. Your files, notes, and passwords are never sent across the internet:

CIPHER
Bank-Grade AES-256 Protection

Every file is scrambled with 256-bit AES-GCM encryption. Even supercomputers cannot open your file without your exact password.

SAFETY
100,000 Rounds of Password Strengthening

We use PBKDF2 with 100,000 computation rounds. This makes automated password guessing (brute-force attacks) computationally unfeasible.

TAMPER PROOF
Built-in Tamper Detection

Each locked file includes an authentication tag. If anyone alters even a single character in your file, CyberVault immediately refuses to open it.
